Who we are

Owners for the long haul. Not a fund, not a flipper.

When we buy a business, we respect what the owner spent years building, and then we build on it. We invest in the team and the tools, sharpen how the work gets done, and go after real growth: new customers, new capabilities, a stronger operation. We try to do right by everyone involved, whether that's a seller, an employee, or a tenant.

We're local, based right here in Union, Kentucky. You're not dealing with an out-of-state fund or a buyer from three states away. The company was founded by an engineer who spent more than two decades building products and leading teams in Northern Kentucky and Cincinnati manufacturing. Read our story.

What we do

Four ways we put capital to work.

Small-business acquisition

We buy established, profitable small businesses from owners who are ready to move on, whether that's retirement or a change of plans. The goal is to build on what works and grow it, not strip it for parts.

Real estate

We buy and manage houses and commercial buildings, mostly places we can improve and hold onto. We'd rather make a property better than flip it.

Partnerships

Sometimes the best deal is a partnership. We're glad to team up with operators or other owners when it makes sense for everyone.

Hands-on ownership

We stay involved. We'd rather be on site, running the business and improving how it works, than manage it from far away.

For sellers

A straightforward path, at your pace.

Selling a business you built is a big decision. Here's how it works with us.

A quiet conversation

We start with coffee, not paperwork. Everything you share stays confidential, and there's no obligation on either side.

A fair, transparent offer

If it's a fit, we'll make an honest offer with clear terms and explain exactly how we got there. No games, no retrading.

A careful handoff

We work the transition on a timeline that suits you, and we treat the name you built and the people behind it with respect.
