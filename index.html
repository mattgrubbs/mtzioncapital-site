Who we are

A steady, long-term owner — not a fund, not a flipper.

Mt. Zion Capital is an independent, family-minded company that buys and operates small businesses and real estate. When we acquire a business, our aim is to steward what someone else worked hard to build: keep the team, serve the customers, and grow it patiently and honestly. We care about doing right by the people on the other side of the table — sellers, employees, tenants, and partners alike.

And we're local — based right here in Union, Kentucky. Not an out-of-state fund or a faraway buyer, but a neighbor who knows this area and intends to be part of it for a long time. We're always glad to meet face-to-face.
