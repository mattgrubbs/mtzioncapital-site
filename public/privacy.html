Privacy

What we collect, and why.

We keep this short and plain. We collect very little, we never sell it, and we use it only to run our business.

Visiting this website

These pages use no cookies, no advertising and no tracking scripts. Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, handles basic connection data to deliver the site and protect it from abuse.

QR codes on our printed materials

Our business cards and capability statement carry short links (mtzioncapital.com/c and mtzioncapital.com/cs). When you open one, we record the date and time, which printed piece it came from, the approximate city, region and country and the name of the network you connected through (for example your company or mobile carrier) as Cloudflare determines them from the connection, your device type, browser and language, and a scrambled, one-way version of your network address so repeat visits can be counted once. We never store the network address itself, and the record does not tell us who you are. You are then taken straight to our government contracting page.

We use these records only to learn which of our materials people find useful. They are kept for up to 13 months and then deleted automatically.

Secure document uploads

If we send you a link to our upload page, we keep the files and the name, company and note you enter so we can do the work you sent them for. Files are stored privately, are never shown to anyone else, and are deleted automatically after the period shown on the upload page.

Email

If you email us, we keep your message and contact details to reply and for our business records.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent any of this information. We share it only with the service providers who run this site and our email for us, or when the law requires it.

Questions

To ask what we hold about you, or to have it deleted, email matthew.grubbs@mtzioncapital.com.
