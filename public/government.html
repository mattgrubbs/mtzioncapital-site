Company overview

Filling the gap for drone and counter-UAS programs.

Mt. Zion Capital LLC provides R&D, engineering and manufacturing partnership services for in-line initiators (EFI, LEEFI and EFDI class) in the defense industry. Our focus is filling the gap in drone and counter-UAS programs, where legacy suppliers have been slow to react.

Our founder spent 7 years designing and building fuze and safe-and-arm hardware at L3Harris and qualified multiple fuze systems that used these detonators and initiators, which gave him a firsthand view of the supplier issues.

Core competencies

What we do.

  • R&D and engineering consulting for initiators and detonators
  • Initiator and detonator mechanical design for producibility
  • Qualification test planning: MIL-DTL-23659, MIL-STD-331, JOTP-52
  • Tolerance analysis (ASME Y14.5), FEA and ECAD-MCAD co-design
  • Technical data packages, configuration control and design reviews
