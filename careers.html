General Manager — Architectural & Ornamental Metals (Confidential Search)

Northern Kentucky Full-time, on-site $110,000–$140,000 base + performance bonus Posted August 2026

Mt. Zion Capital is leading a confidential General Manager search for an established Northern Kentucky company in the architectural and ornamental metals industry.

About the company

This company has spent more than two decades designing, fabricating, and installing custom metalwork across the region — primarily commercial projects for general contractors, institutions, and developers, complemented by select custom residential work. It is profitable, generates multi-million-dollar annual revenue, and runs on a tight-knit team of roughly 15–20 skilled fabricators, installers, project managers, and office staff. The work is genuinely custom — every project is estimated, designed, built, and installed by the same team.

The opportunity

This is a rare seat: full general management of a healthy, established company, with real authority and a structured runway to earn it. You'll spend your first 6–9 months working shoulder-to-shoulder with current senior leadership in a deliberate transition period — learning the customers, the estimating approach, the crews, and the craft — and take on increasing ownership of day-to-day operations as you ramp. From day one this is a decision-making role, not a staff position.

What you'll do

  • Run day-to-day operations across the shop, field crews, and office
  • Own estimating: take-offs from plans and specs, competitive commercial bids, and residential quotes
  • Generate new business — deepen relationships with general contractors and construction managers, get the company on more bid lists, and keep a pipeline of commercial and residential work full
  • Oversee field installations: visit job sites, verify quality and schedule, and keep crews set up to succeed
  • Make the people decisions: hiring, development, accountability, and, when necessary, terminations
  • Build the systems: put scheduling, job-costing, estimating, and process discipline in place so the company runs smoother and is ready to grow
  • Own the P&L: pricing, margins, and the operating budget

What we're looking for

  • 7+ years of progressive leadership in commercial construction, specialty trades, or a subcontractor business — you've run crews, jobs, and budgets
  • Hands-on estimating and bidding experience — you can read drawings, do a take-off, price a job, and defend the number
  • A track record of winning commercial work: comfortable in front of GCs, construction managers, and architects — and equally at ease with builders and homeowners
  • Experience leading teams of 10+ across shop and field
  • An operator's mindset: you build processes, hold standards, and make clear decisions
  • Currently living in the Greater Cincinnati / Northern Kentucky area — this is an on-site role and local candidates only
  • Valid driver's license; comfortable splitting your week between the office, the shop floor, and job sites

Strong pluses: metal fabrication or welding industry background; experience with submittals, AIA progress billing, and retainage; estimating or job-costing software experience; OSHA 30.

Compensation & benefits

  • $110,000–$140,000 base salary, depending on experience
  • Performance bonus tied to company results
  • Health insurance
  • Paid time off and paid holidays

A note on confidentiality: this is a confidential search. The company's identity will be shared with qualified candidates as the process advances, and every application is handled with discretion.
